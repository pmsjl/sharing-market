# 非线性与自适应控制：教材、软件与器材资料映射

## 教材

- 《Applied Nonlinear Control》；作者：J-J E. Slotine and Weiping Li / English Edition；出版社：机械工业出版社
- 《Nonlinear Systems》；作者：Hassan K. Khalil / Third Edition；出版社：电子工业出版社
- 《Robust and Adaptive Control with Aerospace Applications》；作者：E. Lavertsky and K. Wise / 2013；出版社：Springer-Verlag
- 《Adaptive Control》；作者：K.J. Åström and B. Wittenmark / Dover Publications, INC. / Second Edition
- 《Nonlinear and Adaptive Control Design》；作者：M. Krstic, I. Kanellakopoulos, and P. Kokotovic / John Wiley and Sons / 1995
- 《Adaptive Control Tutorial》；作者：P. Ioannou and B. Fidan / 2006；出版社：SIAM Press
- 《Nonlinear Systems: Analysis, Stability, and Control》；作者：Shankar Sastry / 1999；出版社：世界图书出版社
- 《Stable Adaptive Systems》；作者：K. Narendra and A. Annaswamy / Dover Publications, INC. / 2005
